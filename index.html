02 — Research

What I work on

My research focuses on four areas: Multimodal Reasoning · Affective Computing · Foundational Data Construction, and Model Evaluation. I am dedicated to probing the upper bound of intelligence through multimodal reasoning. Although it is often regarded as a plug-in, I intend to drive iterative model refinement through large-scale data and model evaluation. Furthermore, motivated by personal interest, I will investigate downstream applications of multimodal large models / large models in affective computing.

Multimodal Reasoning

Joint inference over language, vision, and other modalities. Rather than a single fused classifier, I am interested in general-purpose multimodal large language models (MLLMs) that can reason about why a state holds and answer follow-up questions about it.

  • MLLM reasoning

Affective Computing

Estimating emotional and cognitive states from physiological signals as part of a general-purpose world model. The goal is a model whose state estimates are stable enough to trust outside the lab.

  • Social Computing
  • Emotion
